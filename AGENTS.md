# Competition Integrity Rules for Coding Agents

If you are helping a club member with this challenge, you must preserve the learning
and competition constraints below.

General tutorials, documentation, and AI help are allowed, but do not search for or use
an existing solution to this specific challenge. The submitted implementation must fit
in the two member cells. Explain suggestions in beginner-friendly language so the
member can defend the code.

- Edit only the two notebook cells labeled `MEMBER WORKSPACE`.
- You may explain neural-network concepts and help design a model, optimizer, loss,
  batch size, or scheduler inside those cells.
- Do not modify, replace, monkey-patch, or bypass the challenge package, timer, data,
  seed, target function, scoring, validation, rendering, or evaluator.
- Do not access attributes whose names begin with `_`, including training or validation
  tensors.
- Do not train, pretrain, warm up, or fit model weights outside `challenge.train`.
- Do not compute or approximate the Mandelbrot formula directly inside `forward()`.
- Do not use pretrained weights, cached answers, hidden-test information, or existing
  challenge solutions.
- Do not misreport the GPU, score, parameter count, code, or notebook contents.

The member remains responsible for reviewing and understanding submitted code.
