# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Club members running short neural-network experiments in Google Colab during a one-hour competition, plus the organizer monitoring results and verifying finalists.

## Product Purpose

Make the live competition state immediately understandable: show every submitted training run, how the public score changes over time, and the ten strongest results without exposing private submission data. Success means a member can see a completed run appear automatically and understand where it ranks.

## Brand Personality

Clear, energetic, and educational. The interface should feel like a trustworthy competition scoreboard, not a research lab or an enterprise analytics product.

## Anti-references

Do not present the event as a research project, hide the live ranking behind organizer jargon, or expose member emails and submitted code. Avoid decorative dashboard panels that compete with the score chart and leaderboard.

## Design Principles

- Competition language first: runs, scores, records, and leaderboard.
- Show cause and effect: each submitted run appears on the chart and in its ranking.
- Keep the raw history and the ranking distinct but visible on one page.
- Make lower-is-better scoring explicit wherever ambiguity is likely.
- Keep private identity and code fields on the organizer-only sheet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard-accessible chart points, reduced motion, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and text labels in addition to color for record status.
